Built for Real-World Retail & Commerce

Everything you need to digitize, manage, and scale your micro-enterprise without complex accounting software.

Smart Customer Khata

Track customer credit and debit balances, transaction histories, and contact info with instant ledger balance tallies and settlement receipts.

📷

Multimodal Bill Scanner

Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision. Snap a photo of handwritten supplier slips or paper bills to instantly parse items, quantities, and totals.

🎙️

Vernacular Voice Khata

Speak naturally in Hindi or Hinglish (e.g., "Ramesh ko 500 rupaye udhaar diye"). Eko’s AI automatically records the ledger entry.

💬

WhatsApp Reminder Studio

Generate polite, friendly, or formal payment reminders in Hindi & English and send them directly to customer WhatsApp chats with 1 tap.

🛡️

Credit Trust Underwriting

Evaluate customer repayment habits with an AI-generated Trust Score (1-100) and suggested safe credit limits to prevent bad debts.

⚡

Offline-First Architecture

Never lose a sale. Local IndexedDB & SQLite storage keeps the app running at full speed without internet, auto-syncing when connectivity returns.
