Privacy Policy

Application: Eko Worker (Eko Field Worker) • Effective Date: August 28, 2026 • Contact: ps9110831@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Welcome to Eko Worker (also referred to as "Eko Field Worker", "we", "our", or "the Application"). Eko Worker is an AI-powered, offline-first operating system designed to empower Indian micro-entrepreneurs, kirana store owners, and field facilitators with smart customer khata ledger management, multimodal receipt scanning, voice accounting, and business advisory tools.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy in compliance with applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and Google API Services User Data Policy.

🔒 Google User Data Commitment: Eko Worker accesses only basic Google profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) to securely authenticate your account. We never sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, and we never use your personal data for advertising or marketing.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide the features of Eko Worker:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your Google Subject Identifier (unique ID), Full Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Business Profile Data: Business name, business category (e.g., Kirana Store, Mobile Recharge, Retail), city/location, and language preference (English, Hindi, Hinglish).
  • Operational & Ledger Data: Customer contact details, credit/debit balances (Khata records), task deadlines, scratchpad notes, and promotional offers created within the app.
  • Multimodal Inputs (Processed On-Demand): Uploaded receipt/bill images and spoken audio voice notes submitted voluntarily for AI transcription and extraction.

3. How We Use Google OAuth Data

Your Google user data is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your identity securely on both the Android Native App and Web Application.
  • Creating and maintaining your isolated business ledger account.
  • Synchronizing your business data across your authorized devices.
  • Displaying your personalized profile greeting and avatar in the application interface.

4. Data Storage & Offline Architecture

Eko Worker utilizes an Offline-First Architecture to ensure uninterrupted operations in low-connectivity environments:

  • Local Device Storage: All customer ledgers, tasks, notes, and session states are cached locally on your device using IndexedDB (for Web/PWA) and Room SQLite (for Android Native App).
  • Cloud Database: When network connectivity is available, data is synchronized over secure TLS/HTTPS to our dedicated, encrypted PostgreSQL database hosted on Render Cloud Infrastructure.
  • Authentication Persistence: Your session profile is cached locally in browser `localStorage` and expires automatically after 30 days of inactivity. Sensitive credentials like Google ID tokens are verified transiently and never persisted in plain text.

5. AI Processing & Google Gemini Integration

Eko Worker integrates Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash generative AI model to provide intelligent productivity superpowers:

  • Multimodal Bill Scanner (OCR): Receipt images sent for OCR are processed transiently to extract line items, prices, and totals. Images are not retained after extraction.
  • Vernacular Voice Khata: Spoken voice notes are parsed into structured ledger credit/debit entries.
  • WhatsApp Studio: Generates respectful, culturally nuanced payment follow-up reminders and marketing promotional copy.
  • Data Privacy in AI: User financial ledger details are never used to train generalized foundation models.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We partner only with industry-standard, secure infrastructure providers:

  • Google Identity Services & Gemini AI: For user authentication and multimodal artificial intelligence processing.
  • Render: For secure cloud hosting of our FastAPI backend application and PostgreSQL database with automated SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Netlify: For global edge hosting and delivery of our static frontend and PWA web application.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your business data only for as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated customer, task, and ledger data, simply email us at ps9110831@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" from your registered Google email address. We will permanently delete all your cloud records within 48 hours.

8. Security Measures

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls:

  • Enforced HTTPS/TLS encryption on all web endpoints and API communications.
  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) and CORS origin whitelisting.
  • Android Native `MIXED_CONTENT_NEVER_ALLOW` policy preventing unencrypted cleartext requests.
  • Database connection pooling with parameterized SQL queries preventing SQL injection.
