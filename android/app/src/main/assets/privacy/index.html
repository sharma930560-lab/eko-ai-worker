Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy describes how Eko AI Operations ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Eko AI Operations (also referred to as "Eko Operations", "we", "our", or "the Application"). Eko AI Operations is an intelligent, offline-first assistant designed to empower Eko's financial-services ecosystem with smart customer operations, transaction monitoring, service activity logging, and operational advisory tools.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy in compliance with applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and Google API Services User Data Policy.

🔒 Google User Data Commitment: Eko AI Operations accesses only basic Google profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) to securely authenticate your account. We never sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, and we never use your personal data for advertising or marketing.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide the features of Eko AI Operations:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your Google Subject Identifier (unique ID), Full Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Business Profile Data: Business name, service role (e.g., DMT Operator, AePS Point, Retail Merchant), city/location, and language preference (English, Hindi, Hinglish).
  • Operational & Service Data: Customer contact details, service activity records, transaction history, operational task deadlines, and support notes created within the app.
  • Multimodal Inputs (Processed On-Demand): Uploaded service bill images and spoken audio voice notes submitted voluntarily for AI transcription and extraction.

3. How We Use Google OAuth Data

Your Google user data is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your identity securely on both the Android Native App and Web Application.
  • Creating and maintaining your isolated operational account.
  • Synchronizing your operational data across your authorized devices.
  • Displaying your personalized profile greeting and avatar in the application interface.

4. Data Storage & Offline Architecture

Eko AI Operations utilizes an Offline-First Architecture to ensure uninterrupted operations in low-connectivity environments:

  • Local Device Storage: All operational data, customers, and session states are cached locally on your device using IndexedDB (for Web/PWA) and Room SQLite (for Android Native App).
  • Cloud Database: When network connectivity is available, data is synchronized over secure TLS/HTTPS to our dedicated, encrypted PostgreSQL database hosted on Render Cloud Infrastructure.
  • Authentication Persistence: Your session profile is cached locally in browser `localStorage` and expires automatically after 30 days of inactivity.

5. AI Processing & Google Gemini Integration

Eko AI Operations integrates Google's Gemini generative AI model to provide intelligent productivity superpowers:

  • Service Document Scanner (OCR): Bill images sent for OCR are processed transiently to extract line items, prices, and totals. Images are not retained after extraction.
  • Vernacular Voice Records: Spoken voice notes are parsed into structured operational activity logs.
  • WhatsApp Studio: Generates professional service updates and support response copy.
  • Data Privacy in AI: Your operational data is never used to train generalized foundation models.

6. Compliance & Contact

We comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For any privacy-related inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at support@eko.co.in.
